# Code of Conduct

Hearth is built by people who give their time for free. Treat them that way.

Criticism of Hearth is welcome. Tell us what's broken, what's confusing, or what you'd do differently, and say why. That's how Hearth gets better.

Personal attacks are not. Insult the owner or any contributor and you're blocked, here and on Discord. No warnings, no debate.

If you want something Hearth doesn't do, open a request or send a pull request. If you'd rather build your own, you're free to.
