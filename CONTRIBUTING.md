# Contributing to Hearth

Thanks for helping. Everyone here follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). A few ways
to take part:

- **Want a panel for an app you run, or a feature on one?** Ask on the
  [panel request board](https://github.com/DragonsOath/hearth/discussions/categories/panel-requests),
  or vote for a request that is already there. [How requests work](docs/HOW_REQUESTS_WORK.md)
  says how they are chosen and what each label means. Saying you can test it before release helps
  a lot. Maintainers: [request triage](docs/REQUEST_TRIAGE.md).
- **Found a bug?** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/DragonsOath/hearth/issues/new/choose),
  including a panel that shows something wrong. Never paste passwords, API keys or tokens.
- **Have an idea for Hearth itself?** Open a feature request from the same page.
- **Writing code?** Read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for the layout, the commands and the rules every
  change follows (approvals are never bypassed, the AI never sees secrets, plain English, tests for
  every feature), and [NATIVE_PANELS_UX.md](docs/design/NATIVE_PANELS_UX.md) before building a
  panel. Work on a branch and open a pull request saying what changed and how it was tested.

## Contributor License Agreement

Outside pull requests are welcome. Before one can be merged, its author signs Hearth's
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) once, by posting the sentence the CLA check asks for as a
comment on the pull request; it covers everything you send afterwards. The CLA is adapted from the
Apache Individual Contributor License Agreement, the standard one many projects use, with Dragon's
Oath as the party receiving the rights:

- You keep the copyright in your contribution.
- You give Dragon's Oath a permanent, free licence to use, change, share and relicense it,
  including commercially (Hearth has free and paid features), and a licence to any of your
  patents it needs.
- You confirm it is your own work, or say clearly where it comes from.

A panel shared from inside Hearth (Panel Builder) gives exactly the same rights; the app shows the
same grant in plain words before you share one.
