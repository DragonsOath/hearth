# GitHub Sponsors profile (DragonsOath)

The text for the DragonsOath Sponsors page, pasted into GitHub's Sponsors settings by the
maintainer account. Keep it in step with `docs/design/PREMIUM.md`; the Founder promise is word for
word (`FOUNDER_PROMISE` in `packages/core/src/premium.ts`).

## Profile

Hearth is a free, self-hosted home for your homelab, with an AI companion that knows your setup.
Sponsoring Dragon's Oath keeps it going, and gives you Hearth Premium: Hearth Connected, Show &
Tell and Curator For You. Everything else in Hearth is free, with no limits.

After you sponsor, open **Claim your Premium key** (the link in your welcome message), sign in
with GitHub, and redeem the code on your Hearth (Settings, Premium, Redeem a code). One GitHub
account covers up to 3 Hearth installations.

## Tiers

**Supporter, $5 a month.** Hearth Premium while you sponsor, and a Supporter role in the Hearth
Discord.

**Founder, $60 once: Launch price, $99 after [date].** Hearth Premium for life, and a Founder
role in the Hearth Discord. (For the first 7 months after launch; then this tier closes and the
regular one opens.)

**Founder, $99 once** (from [date]). Hearth Premium for life, and a Founder role in the Hearth
Discord.

## Our promise to Founders

1. **Premium for life.** Whatever a paying monthly Premium member gets, now or in the future, a
   Founder gets too, at no extra charge, even if Premium later becomes monthly billing only.
2. **The regular Founder price, $99, is never raised.** Founder may be retired at any time, with
   at least 30 days' public notice.
3. **Founders stay Founders.** Everyone who became a Founder before it closes keeps it, forever.

This promise is also in the Hearth Pledge.
